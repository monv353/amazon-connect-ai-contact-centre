# Arunika Telecom customer FAQ (grounding document)

Arunika Telecom is a fictional Indonesian mobile and home-internet provider used for demos.
Everything below is invented demo data. The assistant may only answer from this document.

## Plans and pricing
- Arunika Home Fiber 50 Mbps costs IDR 329,000 per month. 100 Mbps costs IDR 459,000 per month. Prices include 11% VAT (PPN).
- Arunika Mobile Postpaid "Halo 25" includes 25 GB data and unlimited calls to Arunika numbers for IDR 150,000 per month.
- Prepaid data packs can be bought in the Arunika app, at Indomaret or Alfamart, or by dialling *123#.
- Upgrading a Home Fiber plan takes effect within 1 hour and the price difference is pro-rated on the next bill.

## Billing and payment
- Postpaid bills are issued on the 1st of each month and are due on the 20th.
- Customers can pay by bank transfer to a BCA, Mandiri or BNI virtual account, by QRIS, through GoPay or OVO, or at Indomaret and Alfamart.
- A late-payment fee of IDR 25,000 applies if the bill is unpaid after the due date.
- Service is soft-suspended (outgoing only blocked) 10 days after the due date and restored within 1 hour of payment.
- E-invoices (e-faktur) for business customers can be requested in the Arunika app under Billing > Tax invoice.

## Outages and technical support
- Planned maintenance is announced 3 days in advance by SMS and in the Arunika app.
- During a confirmed area outage there is no need to report it again; the app shows the estimated restoration time.
- If a Home Fiber outage lasts more than 24 hours, customers automatically receive a pro-rated credit on the next bill.
- Basic troubleshooting for Home Fiber: restart the ONT (the white modem box) by unplugging it for 30 seconds, then check that the PON light is solid green. A red LOS light means a fibre cut and needs a technician.
- Technician visits are free for line faults. A visit for faults inside the customer's home wiring costs IDR 100,000.

## Account and SIM
- Lost or stolen SIM: call or chat to block it immediately. A replacement SIM is free at any Arunika store; bring your KTP (national ID card).
- SIM registration requires a valid NIK (16-digit national ID number) and KK (family card) number, as required by Indonesian regulation.
- eSIM is available for postpaid customers on supported phones and can be activated in the Arunika app.
- To change the account holder name, visit an Arunika store with both parties' KTP.

## Store hours and contact
- The contact centre is available 24/7 by chat. Voice agents are available 08:00 to 22:00 WIB (Jakarta time), every day.
- Arunika stores are open 10:00 to 21:00 WIB in major malls in Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya, Medan and Makassar.

## Privacy
- Arunika will never ask for your full PIN, OTP code or password by chat, phone or SMS.
- Customers can request a copy of their personal data under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) via the Arunika app under Profile > Privacy.
